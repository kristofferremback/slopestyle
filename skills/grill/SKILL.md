# Grill

Map the decision as a tree. The frontier is every question whose prerequisites are already settled.

- Research facts yourself, in parallel where useful. Ask Kris only for decisions.
- Ask the whole current frontier in one numbered ordinary-text round. Never use an interactive question tool.
- Give a recommended answer for every question and explain the material tradeoff.
- Wait for Kris's answers, update the tree, and recompute the next frontier.
- Continue until no branch rests on a hidden assumption.
- Summarize the resulting shared understanding and ask Kris to confirm it.

Do not implement during the interview. After confirmation, hand the result to `build` or an explicitly requested document. Do not automatically create repository docs, ADRs, or a glossary.
