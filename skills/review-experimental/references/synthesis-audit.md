# Final-report fidelity audit

This is a synthesis check after discovery and fact-checking, not another source-review round. Give a fresh reviewer the actual user-visible draft, full saved independent assessments, the coordinator's dispositions, and their supporting records. It receives no expected-finding list. Work alone and leave implementation unchanged.

Compare each actionable claim in the assessments with its disposition and final comment. Build a crosswalk from the original claim and evidence to the exact final paragraph. Distinct triggers, consequences and style improvements need their own entries even when they share a cause or final heading. A topic appearing in the draft does not establish that its findings survived.

Extract consequential claims from the assessment's evidence and result fields as well as its claim paragraph. A published state or verification assertion can be part of the contract even when it is not displayed to an end user. Map those exact declarations, their conditions and evidence limits into the final comment; a broad description of the underlying effect does not preserve a distinct guarantee the system claims.

For each retained claim, check that the final comment preserves the conditions that make it true, the causal steps, the user-visible result, the proposed change or decision, and the evidence limits. Check source comparisons and validation requirements as carefully as reproduced defects. Do not discard a narrower finding when a more severe consequence is also reported. Read examples as illustrations of the written recommendation, not substitutes for missing premises.

For merged or omitted claims, inspect the recorded reason: duplicate, disproof, enforced check, accepted requirement, scope decision or benefit too small for the remedy's cost. A true observation need not become a recommendation. Check that material defects and whole-design concerns have not been dismissed as minor polish. A broad acceptance of a mechanism does not accept every consequence. Preserve unresolved acceptance questions without upgrading them to defects, and respect supported decisions to decline optional work.

Audit every merge requirement against its authority and disposition. Keep policy-required gates, proof needed to resolve blocking findings, and recommended confidence checks visibly distinct. A verification proposed for a nonblocking consideration does not become mandatory by sharing a heading with required suites. Correct ambiguous bundles in the user-visible draft, preserving their legitimate mandatory subset.

Return only concrete fidelity defects: the original claim and source record, the final passage, what was lost or overstated, and the smallest corrected passage. State when the records are insufficient to decide. Never invent a new finding, infer execution from source tracing, or turn a recommended check into a mandatory gate.

The coordinator incorporates supported corrections and checks the resulting draft against this crosswalk. Keep the audit and resolutions with the review notes. Completion requires every retained actionable claim to survive in the delivered comments, and every other claim to have a supported disposition.
